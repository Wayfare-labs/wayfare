Wayfare

Corridor integrity monitor. Prices a stablecoin → fiat-token corridor across trade sizes, scores every route against an independent reference rate, and states plainly when none are worth taking.

Every figure is fetched live from Horizon pathfinding and an independent rate provider at the moment you press Measure. Nothing is cached, estimated, or interpolated. Wayfare is non-custodial and read-only: it never holds funds, issues tokens, or moves money.
