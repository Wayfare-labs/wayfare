Wayfare prices a stablecoin → fiat-token corridor against an independent reference rate, scores every route against that benchmark, and states plainly when no route is worth taking.

Select a corridor above and press Measure live to fetch a fresh reading, or Show trend to inspect recorded history. The instance may be waking up from sleep — Render free-tier pauses after 15 minutes of inactivity (this usually takes under a minute).

Wayfare is non-custodial and read-only: it never holds funds, issues tokens, or moves money.
