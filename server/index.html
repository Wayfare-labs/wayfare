Wayfare

Corridor integrity monitor. Prices a stablecoin → fiat-token corridor across trade sizes, scores every route against an independent reference rate, and states plainly when none are worth taking.

Wayfare prices a stablecoin → fiat-token corridor against an independent reference rate and states plainly when no route is worth taking.

The instance may be waking up from sleep — Render free-tier pauses after 15 minutes of inactivity. This usually takes under a minute.

Wayfare is non-custodial and read-only: it never holds funds, issues tokens, or moves money.
