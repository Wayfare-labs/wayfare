# NGNC Peg Maintenance Research

Collected public statements and SEP endpoints regarding NGNC issuance and redemption.

- **Date:** 2024-03-01
- **Findings:** NGNC trades at a significant discount on the DEX due to lack of exit bids, but the anchor maintains direct parity redemption via SEP-24 bank transfer mechanisms.
- **Implication:** Corridor cost must account for rail choice (anchor redemption vs DEX market sale).
